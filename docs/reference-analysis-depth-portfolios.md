## What they share (the design system behind "not flat")

1. **The page is a place.** Every hero is a full scene (painted, photographed or 3D), not a layout on a flat colour. The type sits inside the scene.
2. **Scrolling is travel.** The sections are stops on a route: leave the surface, climb, arrive. Each change of section changes the world (sky to space, field to desk).
3. **Real objects are the interface.** A CRT frames the portrait, cassette drawers are the process, a retro device holds the case studies, x-rayed objects are the projects, a shopping cart holds the hobbies. You press the object, not a button next to it.
4. **Three kinds of image are mixed on purpose:** a painted or photographed ground, photoreal cut-out objects with real shadows, and flat UI on top (labels, tags, small buttons).
5. **Type with a material.** The name is made of something (chrome, electronics, glass). Everything else is quiet: one serif, one sans, one mono for small labels.
6. **Small HUD labels in mono** add a second, technical voice: issue numbers, "currently: designing", "loading curiosity 90%".
7. **The loader is a moment** with copy in the world's voice ("Aligning the stars", "Building the island").
8. **Play you can switch off.** Ayush's "Interactive mode" toggle and Vibhor's guided tour give a fast, plain path for recruiters.
9. **Generative touch.** Frances's garden grows where you click. It is the closest to Lana's own garden idea.
10. **Purple is shared** by the two most magical ones (Adya, Ayush), so a purple world fits the genre.

## What this changes in the Night Almanac

The almanac system (light, paper, line, glass) stays, and gets a fifth layer: **depth.**

- **Painted world.** Four painted scenes (Codex): the night garden with a far greenhouse (hero), a stair through glowing flowers (the descent), the inside of the glass conservatory (where the work lives), and the garden at first light (contact). Scrolling travels through them in order.
- **Objects as interface.** Photoreal objects (Codex): an apothecary seed cabinet whose six drawers are the contents, a brass magic lantern that plays the films, a gilded oval frame for the portrait, a violet almanac book for the about section, and a glass bell jar over the product work.
- **Garden that grows where you click** in the hero (Frances's idea, drawn as Lana's own paper flowers, not particles).
- **A loader in the world's voice:** "Waking the garden".
- **A "quiet mode" switch** that stops every effect and shows the plain page, for recruiters and for reduced motion.

What is not copied: Adya's chrome lettering, Sudar's object letters and HUD tracking boxes, Ayush's device, Chi's x-ray bag. The lessons are taken, the signature pieces are not.
