2 · Posters: the easel. The twelve hang small on a line across the sky; choosing one takes it down and flies it onto the easel, big and alone.

3 · Films: the projector. Choosing a clip clicks the projector; its beam throws the film's frame onto the screen. The strip below picks the film.

4 · Products: inside the phone. A pinned stage per app: a film of the real app (this is a frame of the real recording) on the app's own colour, steps that light as you scroll, a rail to jump, and one small working piece below.

5 · About: the hand mirror. The portrait is an engraved line drawing that wakes into the photo where you point (shown half way). The moon calendar sits under the statement.

6 · Contact: the envelope at dawn. The envelope has opened and the letter has slid up; "Copy the address" presses the wax seal.
