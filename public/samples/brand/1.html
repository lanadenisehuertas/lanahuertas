The ideaOne sentence the whole site keeps.

The garden sleeps in one ink. Touch the moon and it wakes in colour.

  • Asleep is lilac dots on night. Awake is full colour, spreading from the moon.
  • Pixel type speaks: names, labels, buttons. A plain sans does the reading.
  • Every section opens with the moon, one phase further on.
  • UI moves in three steps (steps(3)), like a sprite. The wake is one smooth 1.8s ring.
