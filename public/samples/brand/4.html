An almanac of my work, read by the moon.

  • Engraved plates in one cream line on aubergine. Nothing is filled in.
  • Double rules frame everything: cards, buttons, panels. Hover widens the gap.
  • The contents are the phases of the moon, one phase per section, in order.
  • An antique serif names, a copperplate script whispers, a book face reads.
